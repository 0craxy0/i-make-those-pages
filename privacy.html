Privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

Short version: I only get what you send me, I only use it to reply and do your project, and I don't sell it to anyone.

What I get

  • Whatever you put in your email to me, like your name, email address, startup name and project details. The contact form doesn't send anything itself. It just opens your own email app.
  • Anything you send me for the project, like logos, text and images.

What this site collects

No cookies and no ads. The fonts are hosted on this site, not loaded from Google. The "try it" box on the home page runs in your browser only and nothing you type there is sent anywhere.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Visit stats

If you say yes on the banner, the site counts visits using GoatCounter. It doesn't use cookies or track you across other sites. It records things like which page you looked at, where you came from, your browser and roughly which country you're in. I use it to see if anyone's actually visiting. If you say no, nothing is counted.

Your yes or no is saved in your browser so the banner doesn't keep popping up. You can change it any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.

What I do with it

I use your details to reply to you and to make your page. I don't sell or share them, apart from using normal tools to do the work, like my email provider and GitHub.

How long I keep it

As long as I need it for your project and any follow-up. Ask me and I'll delete it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete what I have about you. Email me and I'll sort it. If you're not happy with how I handle it, you can complain to the ICO.
